## What the account's own record says (now computed, `node src/learn.mjs`)

24 Reels, 14 followers, $16.70 total spend. Median 156 views — but the four
best (1229, 1141, 982, 538) all land on the viewer's own life: their health,
their next purchase, their privacy, their pay. The institutional stories
(AI Act, FCC, Congress, robotaxis) sat at or under the median with the only
zero-share records, **and all of them were recognisable** — recognition was
never the differentiator. Followers arrived on hit days: +5 on 08/08, +4 on
09/08, out of +14 lifetime. Retention is 10–30% everywhere with 75% of
viewers gone in the first 3 seconds (the 08-03 curve), so the opening — not
the length — remains the account's one battle.

## What changed

**Editorial.** `proximite` (0.20) joined the scoring weights, paid for out of
stakes and sourceQuality — the measured pattern above, encoded where picks
happen. The lessons digest (`src/learn.mjs`, `state/lessons.json`) runs at
step 2b: top/bottom sendTests side by side, medians by opening surface, theme
and hour, follower movement by day. Thin samples label themselves.

**The gate.** The online loop now fetches evidence on EVERY slide (a
fabricated hook quote could feed the digit pool unfetched — probed live with
`figure: 999999` before the fix); hook/cta evidence without a URL is refused
offline. `tokens()` went Unicode ("vérifiés" no longer shreds to "rifi";
English output byte-identical, ledger fingerprints intact). The result object
carries `verifiedOnline` and the CLI says NOT VERIFIED ONLINE on `--offline`
passes. Bare spelled-out magnitudes ("des milliards") warn for a hand
re-read; digit-adjacent ones stay silent. A Wayback fallback sits in
`fetchPage` — dormant until `web.archive.org` joins the egress allowlist
(probed: `hostname_blocked`) — every archive verification is labelled
VERIFIED_VIA_ARCHIVE and disclosed as a warning. All 30 real specs re-gated
before/after: zero error-level diffs.

**Paid builds.** A $6/day circuit breaker reads `spend.jsonl` before every
purchase (`OOM_DAILY_SPEND_CAP` to override). Veo poll timeouts and failed
downloads leave `video-orphan` receipts instead of invisible charges.
Acquisition runs free-network surfaces (screenshots, photos) before paid ones
(veo, stills), so a walled receipt page kills a build at $0. `veo_N.mp4` and
`still_N.jpg` carry key-sidecar caches like the narration — rebuilds stop
re-buying. The end-card sizes itself instead of throwing after every purchase
was made. ffmpeg gets 420s and one retry on kill only.

**Visual (Hasan's asks, frame-verified).** Captions 104→112 and 78→88 with
caps scaled off the proven fit point; the screenshot-beat band moved from
MarginV 170 — inside Instagram's own bottom UI strip, which is why it read
"too low" on a phone — to 350, and the receipt card is 100px shorter so the
raised band clears it. The title ladder moved up one rung (52-char worst case
still lands on 74). A top-right countdown, 60→1, counts the seconds actually
in the file and zeroes at the cut. It is a flag (`format.mjs COUNTDOWN`) with
a measurement plan: compare 7-day median retention before/after via
`learn.mjs`; if it drops, flip it off in a commit that says so.

**Ops.** `src/prune-media.mjs`: heavy files of Reels published >7 days ago go
(Meta serves its own copy; SHA-pinned URLs serve from history) — executed,
media/ 529→315 MB. `insights.mjs compact`: metrics series 1,171→505 rows —
executed. Both join the Sunday 19:30 vigil. Manual contradictions fixed
(standalone-watch wording, the example JSON that failed its own em-dash rule,
the $0.60 veo price that is really $0.96 at 8s/1080p).
