# Typefaces

All four faces below are static instances cut from Google Fonts variable
originals, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits
redistribution and modification including under a new name.

| file | cut from | instance | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `oom-display.ttf` | Archivo (Omnibus-Type) | wght 900, wdth 92 | the hook card |
| `oom-display-wide.ttf` | Archivo (Omnibus-Type) | wght 900, wdth 125 | the end-card |
| `oom-caption.ttf` | Inter (Rasmus Andersson) | wght 900, opsz 32 | the karaoke |
| `oom-ui.ttf` | Inter (Rasmus Andersson) | wght 700, opsz 32 | handle and credits |

Why these and not the previous pair: Anton is a very condensed poster face and
it read as social-video default rather than as a news desk. Archivo Black is
wider, heavier and calmer at display size; Inter is drawn for screen legibility
at speed and has proper French diacritics, which is what a caption read in
motion on a phone actually needs.
